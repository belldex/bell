Storefronts

Ship your v4 hook from a storefront with verified source and get paid on every swap that runs it. Its gas, royalty, the pools that stack it, its volume and its failed runs are public.

Explore storefronts

Royalties

Paid in the currency the swap moves, as claims held on the PoolManager. Authors claim after seven days. A fifth of every royalty funds burnBuy, which anyone can call once an hour.

Explore royalties

Stakes and failed runs

Every listed v4 hook has 10,000 $V4M staked behind it. Three failed runs in a week burn 10% and remove the hook. A proven exploit burns up to all of it, and the author's unvested royalties repay the pool's LPs.

Session fees by market state

The configured fee follows the verified stock session. Bell, corporate-action and repricing guards can raise it; invalid or unexpected state halts swaps.

01

Regular session

Base LP fee: 0.05%. Healthy active market inputs are required; stale or unknown inputs halt swaps.

02

Opening bell fee

The opening bell fee decays over 300 seconds after supported market open and never undercuts the current session fee.

Corporate-action guard

A stock-token pause or multiplier change invalidates trading until post-boundary data is consistent.
