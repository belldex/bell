A v4 pool gets one hook, forever

The hook is fixed in the pool key when the pool opens, and nothing can be added later. So each pool runs one team's code. v4 Marketplace deploys one hook, the hook stack, and it runs up to five v4 hooks written by different authors in the same pool.

Watch live swaps

Every swap pays every v4 hook it passes through

Each hook sets a royalty from 0 to 10 basis points, with 30 for the whole stack. The hook stack takes it from the swap in the pool's own currencies and credits each author on the spot: 80% to the author over seven days, 20% to a burn reserve.

The hook stack

One v4 hook for every v4 Marketplace pool, running up to five v4 hooks in order. Each runs with a hard gas limit. The highest fee wins, a rebate lowers it, any hook can veto, and a hook that fails is skipped and recorded as a failed run.

Storefronts

Ship your v4 hook from a storefront with verified source and get paid on every swap that runs it. Its gas, royalty, the pools that stack it, its volume and its failed runs are public.

Explore storefronts

Royalties

Paid in the currency the swap moves, as claims held on the PoolManager. Authors claim after seven days. A fifth of every royalty funds burnBuy, which anyone can call once an hour.

Explore royalties

Stakes and failed runs

Every listed v4 hook has 10,000 $V4M staked behind it. Three failed runs in a week burn 10% and remove the hook. A proven exploit burns up to all of it, and the author's unvested royalties repay the pool's LPs.

Four house hooks on day one

The house storefront opens with four v4 hooks that hold no funds. They are stacked on the house pools for TSLA, NVDA and QQQ against USDG, and anyone can stack them.

01

Volatility fee

The fee follows the pool's own recent moves: a moving average of tick changes between swaps sets it between 0.05% and 2%.

Read the formula
02

Anti-snipe

A 25% fee the moment a pool opens, falling to nothing over ten minutes. Made for the v4 Marketplace pool a Pons coin opens beside its Pons pool.

Read the formula
03

Agent rebate

A swap sent by a wallet that holds an ERC-8004 agent identity pays a quarter less fee. Agents find the pools through the hook stack's events.

Read the formula

04

Weekend gap guard

While the stock market is closed, the fee widens to 1% and swaps that push the price outside 2% of the last close are refused.
